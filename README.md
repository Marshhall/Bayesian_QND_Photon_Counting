<h1>Bayesian QND Photon Counting</h1>

<p>
  A study of Bayesian inference applied to quantum non-demolition (QND)
  photon counting. Using experimental measurements of Rydberg atoms
  passing through a microwave cavity, we infer the photon number
  without absorbing the photons.
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Estimate photon-number probabilities using Bayes’ rule.</li>
  <li>Reconstruct photon-number trajectories and observe quantum jumps.</li>
  <li>Explore uncertainty through entropy and mutual information.</li>
</ul>

<p>
  Based on the experiment by <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.3880">Guerlin et al. (2007)</a>.
</p>
